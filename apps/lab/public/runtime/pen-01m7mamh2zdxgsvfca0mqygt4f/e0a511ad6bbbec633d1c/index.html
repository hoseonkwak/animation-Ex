Drag across the card to inspect it. Arrow keys move the box.

How it is built

The cards and their layout view are plain CSS. The layout view is a second copy of the card drawn as boxes, and clip-path: inset() cuts it to the box you drag; every card keeps its layout under visibility, so all of them can be measured. Reactive CSS adds the interaction, with no application JavaScript:

  • Drag across the card: :on(drag(start)) keeps the press point (a drag starts after a short move, so it is event(local-x) minus event(delta-x)), and :on(drag) moves the other corner. Both are length state()s, kept on the stage with clamp(). The arrow keys move the box.
  • element(width) and element(height) measure each part in the layout view. tan(atan2(length, 1px)) turns a length into a number, so counter() can print "52 × 32".
  • Each card button carries its card number and first box in data-* attributes, read with attr(); :on(click) shows the card and places the box, and persist() remembers the card after a reload.
  • Play in the player card is a state() that runs the track, in both copies. The switches and Light | Dark are checkboxes read with :has().
  • Each hint fades after its first use, through a page-wide state() and :when().
